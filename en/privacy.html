1. Information We Collect

The App does not ask you to provide personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or contact details.

The App may collect limited, non-identifying information automatically to operate and improve the App, such as:

  • App launches and basic usage events (e.g., which screens are opened)
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • Purchase events (e.g., whether an in-app purchase was completed)

2. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners (for example, Google AdMob). These partners may use device identifiers or similar technologies to display ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

If you purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase, the App will stop showing non-rewarded ads where applicable. (Some system dialogs or external pages are not controlled by the App.)

3. In-App Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove ads ("Remove Ads"). Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The App does not receive your payment details (such as credit card numbers).

A "Restore Purchases" option is available in the App for users who reinstall the App or switch devices.

4. Analytics and Diagnostics

The App may use analytics and diagnostic tools to understand general usage patterns and improve stability and performance. These tools may collect information such as device model, operating system version, and app interaction events. This information is used only to improve the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
